# 01 · Research — principles from outside and inside the space

> Method: read exemplary work directly (Bruno Simon's portfolio, The Pudding's
> data stories, Teenage Engineering's product/guide surfaces), scan the current
> trend landscape (Awwwards portfolio category, 2025–26 portfolio-trend pieces,
> the glassmorphism/bento saturation debates), and extract principles — never
> surfaces. This document is the taste foundation for Phase 3.

## Where the evidence came from

- **bruno-simon.com** (read live). A drivable Three.js world; the entire résumé is
  diegetic (billboards, objects, a whisper wall); "Behind the scene" openly documents
  the stack (Three.js, Rapier, Howler, fonts), publishes source under MIT, and links
  devlogs. Achievements/timer make exploration countable.
- **The Pudding — "How Bad Is Your Streaming Music?"** (read live). A data piece that
  is genuinely interactive (reads your real Spotify/Apple data), satirical in voice,
  and ruthless about privacy in plain language ("does not save any data").
- **Teenage Engineering OP-1 guides/product pages** (search + known surfaces).
  Instrument-grade restraint: mono type, function-labeled panels, zero ornament;
  the documentation is part of the product's desirability.
- **Trend landscape scans**: Awwwards portfolio/developer categories; 2025–26 pieces
  confirming bento grids and glassmorphism are now the default look; community
  fatigue ("avoid gradient + glassmorphism on your portfolio" is itself a genre now).

## Principles (what the best work actually does)

1. **The world is the navigation.** Bruno Simon: you move, and the portfolio is where
   you end up. Navigation-as-mechanics beats menus for memorability — but it *needs*
   an accessibility path (his has keyboard + text alternatives) to be defensible.
2. **Prove the medium by using it.** The Pudding's judge-your-music only works because
   it really reads real data. The lesson: an interactive claim must run for real.
3. **Document the making, publicly.** Bruno's "Behind the scene" + MIT source turns a
   spectacle into an engineering artifact. Craft you can examine is proof.
4. **Restraint reads as confidence at instrument-grade quality.** TE's panels: every
   mark has a function; desirability comes from precision, not decoration.
5. **Voice is a design material.** The Pudding's dry wit ("sophisticated A.I. judges
   your awful taste") is why it spreads. Copy is craft, not filler.
6. **Honesty as a feature, stated once, plainly.** Privacy notes, "does not use real
   A.I." disclaimers — the best pieces say what they are in one line, then stop.
7. **Countable exploration.** Achievements, timers, scores: the visitor's curiosity is
   *rewarded mechanically*, not just visually.
8. **Everything degrades.** The strong pieces still answer on text-only, keyboard-only,
   slow-network reads. Spectacle is a layer over a complete document.

## Patterns worth adapting (not cloning)

- Diegetic interface elements (the UI belongs to the world, not to a chrome layer).
- The public "how this is built" layer as a first-class page, not a footer link.
- Real-data interactions that make the visitor *part of the evidence*.
- A single strong metaphor carried from first pixel to 404 page.
- Editorial pacing: guided path for the impatient, depth for the curious.

## The saturated cliché list (verified live, 2025–26 landscape)

Bento grid of everything · glassmorphism cards · purple/blue gradient heroes ·
floating blobs/orbs/particles · neon-on-black "futuristic dev" look · giant
"HI, I'M X 👋" typography · skill-percentage bars · logo walls · project card grids

"passionate/crafting digital experiences" copy · AI-generated icon sets ·
WebGL for decoration instead of meaning · dark-mode-by-default with glow.

## Synthesis — the seam none of them occupy

Every exemplary portfolio proves its **design** craft by being a designed object.
None of them prove the **engineering** craft by *running a system* — the kind of
system a full-stack + mobile engineer actually ships: an API with state, a client,
a traceable request path, graceful degradation. And none of them make the portfolio's
own operation the evidence ("you are request #, here's the path your visit took").
For an engineer whose range spans backend, web, and mobile, the unused format is the
one the discipline itself uses daily: **running systems, observable in production.**

Second seam: the conversation seed (E) exists nowhere in the space as a *woven-in*
guide with a hard fallback — the few AI-chat portfolios bolt a bubble to a corner.
A digital self that is a *wayfinding layer over real evidence*, with a designed
non-conversational path, is genuinely unoccupied territory.

Both seams point at one direction; carried into `03-concepts.md`.
