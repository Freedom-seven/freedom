# 01 · Creative Vision — Inspection Copy

## The central idea

The portfolio is **issued as an inspection copy of itself** — the term museums use
for documents distributed to be examined. It is not styled like an artifact;
it behaves like one: every element can be asked what it is, and the page
answers with testimony that is **measured at runtime** rather than recited.

The defining interaction is **the loupe**: arm it (press `I`, or the arming tab),
then hover, click, or focus anything that carries testimony. A chip opens with
four fields:

| Field | Nature | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **what** | authored description + live measurement | "3 decisions each stated with its cost; 2 evidence links; block measured 812px just now" |
| **why** | the authored decision, in plain language | "Work should be evaluated the way engineers actually evaluate it." |
| **cost** | what the decision gave up | "Case files demand reading; there is no skimming a decision." |
| **where** | outward evidence link | repo, deployment |

## The emotional target

The intended progression, in the visitor's own head:

1. "What am I looking at?" — a paper document, not an app.
2. "Oh, I understand." — chapters, exhibits, a record.
3. "Wait… it can do that?" — the lens opens testimony.
4. "That's real." — the measurements change when the viewport changes.
5. "Someone put serious thought into this." — the costs are admitted.

## Principles (governing laws)

1. **Truth is the material.** If the page says it measures, it measures. If it
   names a token, it reads the CSSOM. If it cites a project, the claim was
   verified against the live repository. The content truth audit caught three
   inaccuracies in our own drafts (see 06) — the system worked.
2. **One level of meta, then stop.** The loupe can inspect the loupe. It
   refuses further regress; infinite meta is a gimmick.
3. **Restraint over spectacle.** One color (verdict green), one material
   (paper), two type voices (serif, mono), one duration (190ms). Nothing glows.
4. **Accessibility is not a mode.** Focus reveals testimony whether or not the
   lens is armed. Hover is the optional layer; keyboard is the guaranteed one.
5. **The visitor owes nothing.** Inspection is discovery, never a gate. A
   visitor who never arms the lens gets a complete editorial portfolio.
6. **Costs are published.** Every meaningful decision is paired with its
   trade-off — the most senior signal available, and the least common.

## What makes it recognizable

Remove the name and profession, and what remains is unmistakable: *a website
that can be asked about itself and always answers truthfully, because it
measures itself rather than reciting.* That is an interaction architecture,
not a skin.
