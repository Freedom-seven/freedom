# 02 · Directions and Selection

Three directions were developed before implementation. Two were rejected.

## Direction A — "The Engineer's Ledger" (rejected as concept, retained as atmosphere)

A portfolio *styled as* an inspection record: paper, ink, rules, annotations.
Strong art direction, but ultimately a **visual language**, not a behavior.
Remove the paper metaphor and nothing about the interaction model survives.
Demoted to the aesthetic substrate of the winning direction.

## Direction B — "Inspection Copy" (selected)

The portfolio as a **self-inspecting artifact** (see 01 and 05). Chosen because
its interaction model is made of truth: runtime measurement, admitted costs,
evidence links, a session record that dies rather than persist.

## Direction C — "The Career Instrument" (rejected)

The career composed as a playable score; projects as movements; optional Web
Audio. Genuinely different — and rejected on the brief's own tests: the
mechanic demonstrates audio engineering (not the work, not the stack), carries
almost no technical evidence, is hostile in offices and on mobile data, and
drifts toward exactly the "impressive through effects" temptation the brief
forbids. It survives as a footnote.

## The review that shaped the final concept

The first approval round rejected the initial plan with a precise diagnosis:
it changed how the portfolio **looks**, not how it **works**. The second round
demanded the concept pass this test:

> Remove the name and profession. Is the remaining experience still distinct?

Direction B passes because its identity is behavioral. The loupe, the
testimony schema, the honest gap, the archive, and the session record are all
the same gesture — a document that vouches only for what it can verify — and
that gesture is the portfolio's argument about its author.
